Project CHENJ

Capital markets Harvesting & Event Network for Jurisdiction intelligence

A Python intelligence pipeline connecting Nairobi Securities Exchange activity with the news and public notices that can move a market.

Read the field note +

CHENJ (Capital markets Harvesting & Event Network for Jurisdiction intelligence) gathers NSE-listed equities and market summary data, collects exchange announcements and recent Kenya Gazette issues, and can extract text from Gazette PDFs. It links relevant notices to listed companies, applies transparent keyword-based impact scoring, then produces dated CSV datasets and a consolidated PDF research report.
